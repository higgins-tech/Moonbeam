GLMR

The native token of the Moonbeam Network, migrating 1:1 to a new Base ERC-20 contract as part of the network relaunch.

MOVR

MOVR

The native token of the Moonriver Network. Bridges via Moonbeam in two steps: to xcMOVR, then to Base ERC-20 MOVR.

Action Steps

Before You Migrate

ALL FAQS ▸
STEP 01
Required First

Exit On-Chain Positions

Withdraw any funds deployed in protocols on Moonbeam or Moonriver — liquidity positions, lending markets, staking contracts, and other DeFi protocols. Once a chain winds down, funds left in on-chain protocols may become permanently inaccessible.

STEP 02
Bridge Tokens

Bridge via the Migration Portal

Use the official migration portal to bridge GLMR directly, or MOVR in two hops via xcMOVR. The migration is 1:1 with no fee. Always verify contract addresses before confirming any transaction.

STEP 03
Confirm & Verify

Verify Your New Balance

Once bridged, confirm your Base ERC-20 balance matches what you migrated. Keep an eye on official channels for further communications as the migration progresses toward the deadline.
